ReplyKeys Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

ReplyKeys is an iPhone keyboard that drafts replies using the AI service you choose. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. ReplyKeys has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers that receive your data. The developer never sees your messages, drafts, screenshots or API keys.

What stays on your device

  • Your API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Your settings (tone, theme, layout, default AI, daily free-reply count) are stored locally on your device.

What is sent to your AI provider

When you tap Paste & Reply, Rewrite or Fix, ReplyKeys sends the text you copied (or a screenshot you copied) or the draft you typed, along with your tone and style choices and your API key, directly from your phone to the provider you selected: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), xAI (Grok) or Perplexity. Nothing is sent until you tap one of those buttons, and nothing is sent to anyone else.

That provider handles the content under its own terms and privacy policy, and your API key is billed by that provider. Please review their policies before use.

Full Access

iOS requires "Allow Full Access" for a keyboard to connect to the internet and read the clipboard. ReplyKeys uses it only to send your request to the AI provider you chose and to read what you copy when you tap Paste & Reply. ReplyKeys does not log or store what you type.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

ReplyKeys is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
