ReplyKeys Support

ReplyKeys puts your own AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok or Perplexity) on your keyboard for tone-matched replies, rewrites and typo fixes.

Getting started

  1. Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard > ReplyKeys.
  2. Tap ReplyKeys, then turn on Allow Full Access.
  3. In any text field, switch to ReplyKeys with the globe key, tap the gear, and paste an API key for your AI.
  4. Copy a message, then tap Paste & Reply.

Common questions

Where do I get an API key? Tap the link icon next to your AI in the keyboard's settings. An API key is billed by the provider and is separate from a chat subscription.

Is my data private? Yes. See the privacy policy.
